# MORA — Collective by Mora

Shopify store: d1xg7s-q3.myshopify.com
Domain: collectivebymora.com

Dawn-based theme for the first release. Connect main in Shopify as an unpublished theme after the bootstrap workflow completes.

## Store setup
- Enable Shopify customer accounts. Account creation does not imply marketing consent.
- Create collections with handles new-drop and all; populate real products, sizes, prices, stock and photos.
- Add Shop All, New Drop, The Collective and Account navigation.
- Customize the MORA launch section with an owned campaign photograph.
- Configure shipping, payment, returns and contact information before launch.

## Proposed loyalty rules (confirm before advertising)
Insider: at least 2 qualifying purchases OR 150 store-currency units spent.
VIP: at least 5 qualifying purchases OR 500 spent. VIP takes precedence.
Use Shopify customer segments and Flow to synchronize tags MORA_INSIDER/MORA_VIP, removing stale tier tags. Reevaluate after paid orders and refunds/cancellations. Test boundaries and refunds before enabling.

## Early access
The theme is presentation, not purchase authorization. Configure and test an access-control solution before making reserved products purchasable. Test direct product URLs, cart additions and checkout as a guest, Member, Insider and VIP. Review other enabled sales channels. Do not advertise guaranteed restricted access until these checks pass.

Suggested release: VIP 48 hours before public, Insider 24 hours before public. Initial members can receive launch announcements only if they opted into marketing.

## Bootstrap
The included workflow imports the official Dawn source into the empty repository, preserves its license, and installs MORA sections and homepage. It does not publish a Shopify theme or install apps. Record upstream revision in DAWN_VERSION.txt.
